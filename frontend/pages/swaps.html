How swaps are chosen

  1. Safe for you – your allergies, diet type, conditions and prescriptions.
  2. Same food group – protein for protein, grain for grain.
  3. Real local price – with its source and date. No guessed prices.
  4. Cheaper – for about the same amount of food.

Swaps are suggestions. DietRx is a decision-support system and does not replace professional medical advice.
